SCIENTIFIC INSTRUMENTS & PAYLOAD

MAST HEAD

Mastcam-Z & SuperCam

Dual multispectral stereo zoom cameras with 3D color imaging and high-speed video. SuperCam fires pulsed infrared lasers to vaporize rock targets up to 7 m away, analyzing elemental chemistry via LIBS and Raman spectroscopy.

CHASSIS INTERIOR

MOXIE (Mars Oxygen ISRU Experiment)

Solid oxide electrolysis unit that extracts pure O₂ from Mars' 95% CO₂ atmosphere at 800°C. Completed 16 successful runs, generating 122 grams of oxygen (~9.8 g/hr at 98% purity).

ROBOTIC TURRET ARM

PIXL & SHERLOC / WATSON

PIXL uses micro-focus X-ray fluorescence to map elemental chemistry at sub-millimeter scales. SHERLOC utilizes deep-UV fluorescence and Raman spectroscopy to detect organic compounds and potential biosignatures in Jezero lakebed strata.

AFT UNDERBELLY

RIMFAX (Radar Imager for Mars' Subsurface)

Ground-penetrating radar operating between 150–1200 MHz. Sounded structural geology down to >10 m depth beneath Jezero Crater floor, discovering tilted sedimentary layers and volcanic basements.

METEOROLOGY

MEDA (Mars Environmental Dynamics Analyzer)

Continuous atmospheric telemetry: air/ground temperature, atmospheric pressure, relative humidity, UV radiation, and wind velocity.
